# Ice Cream Science

The [`sci-cream` crate](https://github.com/ramonrsv/sci-cream/tree/main/packages/sci-cream) has a
comprehensive and growing ice cream science [knowledge
base](https://docs.rs/sci-cream/latest/sci_cream/docs/index.html). The information there has been
meticulously researched and cites formal sources like published peer-reviewed literature and
reference texts, as well as other well-regarded resources. It is meant to be an in-depth reference.

In contrast, the information here is meant to be supplemental and accessible to new home cooks just
starting to explore the world of ice cream science. It is less in-depth and much less formal, also
allowing me to incorporate the knowledge that I have acquired from my own experiments. The tradeoff
in rigor means this information is more likely to contain mistakes or inaccuracies, so take it with
a grain of salt. Where appropriate, it links to the relevant sections in the crate's [knowledge
base](https://docs.rs/sci-cream/latest/sci_cream/docs/index.html).
