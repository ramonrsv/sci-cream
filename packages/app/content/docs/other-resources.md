# Other Resources

This project would not have been possible without the great work that others have generously shared.
Be it ice cream spreadsheets, calculators, blogs, books, online communities, etc. They have been
instrumental as tools to develop ice cream recipes, as references during the early days of
developing Sci-Cream software, and most importantly as a deep well of ice cream science knowledge.

This page collects all such resources that I have used or that I am aware of. Many of them represent
fantastic work that I have a lot of respect for, and I would highly encourage you to give them a
visit!

## Sources

These are some of the online sources, primarily blogs, that I have used extensively throughout my
ice cream making journey, and that I continue to use on a regular basis. Some of their ice cream
science articles and reference recipes are collected in these pages, grouped by source:

- [Science Articles](/docs/other-resources/science)
- [Reference Recipes](/docs/other-resources/recipes)

### Underbelly

<img src="/images/docs/underbelly_logo.png" alt="Underbelly Logo" width="400"
style="display: block; margin: 0 auto; background: black">

[under-belly.org](https://under-belly.org/category/ice-cream/)'s "Standard Base" recipe is arguably
_the_ standard base recipe - it's certainly the foundation for a lot of the recipes I have
developed, as are the [strawberry sorbet](https://under-belly.org/sample-sorbet-recipe/), [Grand
Marnier](https://under-belly.org/booze-flavored-ice-cream/), and
[chocolate](https://under-belly.org/ice-cream-flavor-chocolate/) recipes. Some of the best ice
creams I have ever had were when I was making these. The ice cream blog has a lot of excellent ice
cream science information that I still visit often. I could not recommend this source enough.

[Science](/docs/other-resources/science#underbelly) ·
[Recipes](/docs/other-resources/recipes#underbelly)

### Ice Cream Science

<img src="/images/docs/ice_cream_science_logo.webp" alt="Ice Cream Science Logo" width="400"
style="display: block; margin: 0 auto">

[icecreamscience.com](https://www.icecreamscience.com/)'s blog has a lot of informative, seemingly
well researched, and very well cited ice cream science knowledge articles. I have discovered many of
the sources that I have used for the [reference
knowledge](https://docs.rs/sci-cream/latest/sci_cream/docs/index.html) base from their posts. Their
recipes were the motivation for the development of the evaporation feature - they are formulated
based on post-evaporation compositions.

[Science](/docs/other-resources/science#ice-cream-science) ·
[Recipes](/docs/other-resources/recipes#ice-cream-science)

### Dream Scoops

<img src="/images/docs/dreamscoops_logo.gif" alt="Dream Scoops Logo" width="400"
style="display: block; margin: 0 auto">

[dreamscoops.com](https://www.dreamscoops.com/home/)'s wonderful [Ice Cream

[Science](/docs/other-resources/science#dream-scoops) ·

### icecreamcalc.com

<img src="/images/docs/icecreamcalc_banner.png" alt="icecreamcalc.com Banner" width="400"
style="display: block; margin: 0 auto">

[icecreamcalc.com](https://icecreamcalc.com)'s calculator software and science knowledge articles
were an instrumental reference during the early days of Sci-Cream - I ~~shamelessly stole~~ borrowed
a lot of their methodology and ingredient composition values for the [Apps Script
predecessor](/blog/2026-04-27-welcome#background) of this project. I don't use it anymore these
days, but their desktop ice cream calculator is a great piece of software that I encourage you to
try out. I think they also have a [web app](https://www.icecreamcalc.app/) now, though I've not had
a chance to try it.

[Science](/docs/other-resources/science#icecreamcalccom) ·
[Recipes](/docs/other-resources/recipes#icecreamcalccom)

## Calculators

These are a handful of ice cream spreadsheets and calculators that I am aware of. Dream Scoops' [ice
cream calculator spreadsheet](https://www.dreamscoops.com/ice-cream-science/ice-cream-calculator/)
was the first that I ever used, and where the [Apps Script
predecessor](/blog/2026-04-27-welcome#background) of this project started from.
[icecreamcalc.com](https://icecreamcalc.com/) is a great desktop calculator, which I used
extensively during the early days of Sci-Cream. I've not yet had a chance to fully explore all of
its features, nor the other entries in this list.

- [icecreamcalc.com](https://icecreamcalc.com/)
- [Ice Cream Science spreadsheet](https://www.icecreamscience.com/blog/commercial-ice-cream-amp-gelato-mix-calculation-spreadsheet)
- [Dream Scoops spreadsheet](https://www.dreamscoops.com/ice-cream-science/ice-cream-calculator/)
- [Gelatodemy](https://gelatodemy.com/en/ice-cream-balancing-software/)
- [Dairy Science Mix Calculator](https://www.dairyscience.info/calculator1/index.php)
- [Owl Software](https://owlsoft.com/)

## Books

List of books for home ice cream makers:

- _Ice Cream_, 7/8th Edition, Goff & Hartel
- _The Science of Ice Cream_, C. Clarke
- _I Segreti del Gelato (Secrets of Ice-Cream)_, A. Corvitto
- _Hello, My Name Is Ice Cream_, D. Cree

Others that I have not used enough yet:

- _Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams at Home_, J. Britton Bauer
- _The Perfect Scoop_, D. Lebovitz
- _Frozen Food Science and Technology_, J. A. Evans (ed.)
- _Frozen Desserts_, F. Migoya
- _Gelato Messina: The Recipes_, N. Palumbo

More technical books about food chemistry:

- _Optimising Sweet Taste in Foods_, W. J. Spillane
- _Glucose Syrups: Technology and Applications_, P. Hull
- _Food Chemistry_, 4th Edition, H.-D. Belitz, et al.
- _Alternative Sweeteners_, 4th Edition, L. O'Brien Nabors
- _Food Process Engineering Principles and Data_, M. Lewis
- _Perry's Chemical Engineers' Handbook_, 8th Edition, R. H. Perry & D. W. Green
