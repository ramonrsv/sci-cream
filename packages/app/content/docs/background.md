# Background

Back in 2024, I bought my first ice cream machine on a whim - I'd always loved ice cream and wanted
to try making my own. Following some recipes online went reasonably well, but trying to develop my
own was an unmitigated disaster - I made a "protein" ice cream that had the consistency of tar. So I
did a bit of research, which led me to discover the world of ice cream science and calculators. I've
been obsessively going down that rabbit hole ever since.

I've learned a lot while developing the various software packages and my own recipes, all of which I
have found very rewarding. There have been some successes - I'm pretty happy with the [`sci-cream`
crate](/docs/overview#sci-cream-crate). And there have been many failures - when making avocado ice
cream, do not cook the avocado, _seriously_. I've loved all of it just the same. I have come to
appreciate good ice cream in a way that I would not have imagined. I'm always on the lookout for new
shops, and delighted on the rare occasions that I find a good one here in the Toronto area.

The _Sci-Cream_ project is a collection of everything I have developed and learned throughout this
journey. By sharing it, I hope to convince you of the joy there is to be had in ice cream making,
and to help you along should you decide to join in, as I have been helped by the generous work of
many [others](/docs/other-resources#sources).

I have ambitious plans for Sci-Cream; I hope to continue improving the software, expanding the
knowledge base, conducting experiments, and more. I will share all of it here.
