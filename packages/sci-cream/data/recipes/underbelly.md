## Standard Base

```json
{
  "author": "Underbelly",
  "recipe": [
    ["Whole Milk", 360],
    ["Heavy Cream", 360],
    ["Skimmed Milk Powder", 55],
    ["Egg Yolk", 36],
    ["Sucrose", 70],
    ["Dextrose", 25],
    ["Invert Sugar", 15],
    ["Locust Bean Gum", 0.8],
    ["Guar Gum", 0.4],
    ["Lambda Carrageenan", 0.2],
    ["Salt", 0.7]
  ]
}
```

[Underbelly's Standard Base](https://under-belly.org/basic-ice-cream-recipe-examples/) recipe,
posted 2016-05-25, visited 2026-05-7.

'Whole Milk' is quoted as 3.3%, which I assume is 3.25% rounded up, consistent with Sci-Cream's
definition. 'Heavy Cream' is quoted as 36%, consistent with Sci-Cream's definition. The 'Egg Yolk'
amount is a whole multiple of 18g large egg yolks. The stabilizers are the 4:2:1 'Underbelly General
Purpose Stabilizer Blend', total 1.4g.

The 15g of 'Invert Sugar' can be replaced by 6g each of 'Dextrose' and 'Fructose', as per
[this](https://under-belly.org/basic-ice-cream-recipe-examples/#comment-709) and
[this](https://under-belly.org/basic-ice-cream-recipe-examples/#comment-12) comment, which is much
easier to work with.

```json
    ["Guar Gum", 0.6],
    ["Lambda Carrageenan", 0.4],
    ["Salt", 1.2],
    ["Vanilla Extract", 10]
  ]
}
```

[Underbelly's "Double Origin" Chocolate Ice
Cream](https://under-belly.org/ice-cream-flavor-chocolate/) recipe, posted 2018-09-25, visited
2026-06-16.

Cocoa powder is assumed to be 22% fat, typical of the brands mentioned, e.g. Valrhona, and agrees
with the stated total fat content. Chocolate is assumed to be 70% dark chocolate, in the middle of
the stated 67-72% range. The stabilizers are a 4:3:2 blend, total 1.8g, and 2g of 'Soy Lecithin' -
this does not match any of Underbelly's listed stabilizer blends, though it does match the 'Light
Variation' blend. The resulting composition agrees with the stated 'Analysis' - except POD and PAC.

## Bourbon Smash Ice Cream

```json
{
  "author": "Underbelly",
  "recipe": [
    ["Whole Milk", 340],
    ["Heavy Cream", 360],
    ["Skimmed Milk Powder", 55],
    ["Egg Yolk", 36],
    ["Sucrose", 85],
    ["Invert Sugar", 12],
    ["Locust Bean Gum", 0.8],
    ["Guar Gum", 0.4],
    ["Lambda Carrageenan", 0.2],
    ["Salt", 1],
    ["Lemon Zest", 4],
    ["Lemon Juice", 24],
    ["Bourbon Whiskey", 65],
    ["Angostura Aromatic Bitters", 2],
    ["Fresh Spearmint", 12],
    ["Peppermint Essential Oil", 0.1]
  ]
}
```

[Underbelly's Bourbon Smash Ice Cream](https://under-belly.org/booze-flavored-ice-cream/) recipe,
posted 2016-12-16, visited 2026-06-21.

The stabilizers are the 4:2:1 'Underbelly General Purpose Stabilizer Blend', total 1.4g. 'Lemon
Zest' and 'Fresh Spearmint' may only partially contribute to the composition, depending on the
preparation, e.g. if the mint is infused rather than blended into the mix. 'Bourbon' is listed as
43%, which Sci-Cream's 'Bourbon Whiskey' matches, but other bourbons can range from 40-45% ABV; one
can substitute in 'X% ABV Spirit' to match the specific bourbon used. The recipe states "very fresh
mint leaves" and "mint essential oil" without specifying a variety - spearmint is commonly used in
cocktails, and peppermint essential oil is most common.
